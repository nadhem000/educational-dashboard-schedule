Consonant Clusters

Master groups of consonants that appear at the beginning or end of words

Consonant clusters are two or more consonants together without a vowel between them. English often combines sounds like "str", "spl", "nt", or "lp". Practice these tricky combinations, then test your ear with the quiz!
