Long Vowels

Master the long vowel sounds: /eɪ/, /iː/, /aɪ/, /oʊ/, /uː/

Long vowels say their name! They are longer, tenser sounds produced with a more stretched mouth shape. Practice listening and repeating each word below, then challenge yourself with the quiz.

A /eɪ/ as in "cake"

The sound is like saying the letter A. Lips spread, tongue moves up. Click to listen.

E /iː/ as in "tree"

Tongue high and front, lips slightly spread. A long "ee" sound. Click to hear.

I /aɪ/ as in "kite"

Starts with an open mouth and glides to a high front position. Like saying "eye".

O /oʊ/ as in "bone"

Lips rounded, tongue moves back and up. A long "oh" sound. Click to listen.

U /uː/ as in "flute" or /juː/ as in "cube"

Lips rounded tightly. Sometimes preceded by a /j/ sound. Click to hear examples.
