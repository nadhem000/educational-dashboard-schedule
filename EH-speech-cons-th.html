TH Sound

Master the two "th" sounds: voiceless /θ/ and voiced /ð/

The TH sound is one of the most distinctive English consonants. There are two versions – voiceless (like in "think") and voiced (like in "this"). Place your tongue between your teeth and push air out. Practice with the words below, then test your ear with the quiz!

/θ/

Voiceless TH

No vocal cord vibration. Just air passing between tongue and teeth. Click the words to hear them.

/ð/

Voiced TH

Vocal cords vibrate – it feels like a gentle buzz. Click to listen.
