Syllable Rhythm

Master the music of English: stressed and unstressed syllables

English is a stress‑timed language. This means some syllables are longer, louder, and higher in pitch, while others are reduced. Getting the rhythm right makes you sound more natural. Below you'll explore common stress patterns, then test your ear.
