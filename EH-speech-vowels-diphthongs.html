Diphthongs

Master the gliding vowel sounds that move from one sound to another

Diphthongs are vowel sounds where your mouth moves from one position to another within the same syllable. They give English its natural flow. Practice each diphthong by clicking the words below, then test your ear with the quiz!

/eɪ/

as in "day"

Starts with /e/ and glides to /ɪ/. Common spellings: "ay", "ai", "a_e".

/aɪ/

as in "my"

Mouth opens wide then moves to a high front position. Often spelled "i_e", "igh", "y".

/ɔɪ/

as in "boy"

Starts with an open /ɔ/ and moves quickly to /ɪ/. Spelling: "oy" or "oi".

/aʊ/

as in "now"

Mouth opens wide and then closes to a rounded /ʊ/. Spellings: "ow", "ou".

/oʊ/

as in "go"

Starts at /o/ and moves to /ʊ/. Found in words with "o_e", "oa", "ow".
