Learn the two main intonation patterns: rising (↗) and falling (↘)

In English, the melody of your voice changes the meaning. Yes/No questions usually end with a rising intonation (↗), while Wh‑questions (who, what, where, when, why, how) end with a falling intonation (↘). Practice with the examples below and then test your ear!
