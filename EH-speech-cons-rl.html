R / L Contrast

Learn to hear and say the difference between /r/ and /l/

The English /r/ and /l/ can be tricky for many learners. /r/ uses a curled tongue that doesn't touch the roof of the mouth, while /l/ uses the tongue tip against the gum ridge. Practice with the words and minimal pairs below, then test your ear!

/r/

R Sound

Tongue curls back, lips slightly rounded. No tongue contact with the roof. Click to listen.

/l/

L Sound

Tongue tip touches the gum ridge behind the upper teeth. Air flows around the sides. Click to listen.
