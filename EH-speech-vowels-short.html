Short Vowels

Master the five short vowel sounds: /æ/, /ɛ/, /ɪ/, /ɒ/, /ʌ/

Short vowels are the most common vowel sounds in English. They are quick, crisp sounds produced with a relaxed tongue. Practice listening and repeating each word below, then test yourself with the quiz!

A /æ/ as in "cat"

This sound is made with a wide mouth, tongue low and forward. Click the words to hear them.

E /ɛ/ as in "bed"

Mouth slightly open, tongue mid‑height, lips relaxed. Click to listen.

I /ɪ/ as in "sit"

A short, relaxed sound. Tongue is high but not tense. Click to hear.

O /ɒ/ as in "hot"

Mouth open, tongue low and back. In American English, it's closer to /ɑ/. Click to listen.

U /ʌ/ as in "cup"

Tongue mid‑central, mouth slightly open. A very relaxed "uh" sound.
